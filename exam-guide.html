What the credential proves

The credential is an independent check that you can architect Claude-based solutions in production. It tells employers, clients and teams that you can own, or significantly contribute to, the full lifecycle of a Claude-powered system.

Built for

Mid- to senior-level people who work between business requirements and technical implementation.

  • Solution architects
  • AI/ML engineers
  • Technical leads
  • Senior software engineers

Not built for

Anyone without experience designing end-to-end AI systems.

  • Entry-level developers
  • Casual users of Claude-based apps
  • Purely non-technical roles
  • Prompt writing with no system design responsibility
  • Financial services
  • Healthcare
  • Retail
  • Technology
  • Education
  • Government
What a certified architect can doSeven capabilities, from prototyping to explaining decisions to stakeholders
  • Design and prototype AI-driven solutions that address real business problems
  • Select appropriate models, architectures and API patterns for specific use cases
  • Implement prompt engineering and context strategies to guide model behavior
  • Integrate Claude into production systems using APIs, orchestration tools and data pipelines
  • Apply evaluation, monitoring and observability practices to ensure solution quality
  • Incorporate security, compliance and governance considerations into system design
  • Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders and communicate architectural decisions effectively
The minimally qualified candidate (MQC)The person the passing score is set for, and the experience the guide recommends

The MQC designs, implements and governs Claude-powered solutions in production. They combine an engineering mindset with real deployment experience and turn business requirements into scalable, secure and reliable architectures.

They can design end-to-end systems that cover prompt and context engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), API integration, orchestration and evaluation. They understand the trade-offs between cost, latency, performance, safety and maintainability.

Recommended experience · tick what you already have

There are no prerequisites. The experience above is recommended, not required. The credential is awarded on exam performance alone.

Scoring, booking and rules

How scoring worksScaled 100–1,000. You pass at 720, measured against a fixed standard.
FailPass
1007201,000

The exam is criterion-referenced. Every candidate is measured against the same fixed standard, so other candidates' results never affect yours.

Trained subject matter experts set the 720 cut score in a formal standard-setting study, based on the performance expected of a minimally qualified candidate.

Your score report shows pass or fail, your scaled score, and your percent correct in each domain. The domain percentages are feedback only. Pass or fail depends on the total scaled score.
